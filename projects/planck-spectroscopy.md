# Planck Spectroscopy

I worked with the Kats Optics Research Group to co-develop Planck Spectroscopy, a novel spectroscopy method to measure spectral emissivity without the need to use wavelength-selective optical components. 
I ran stochastic simulations in MATLAB to test the effects of noise on our technique.
